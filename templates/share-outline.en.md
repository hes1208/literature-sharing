## 02 Chinese translation of the abstract

Translate the full original abstract with accurate terminology, quantities and qualifications. Never compress it.

## 03 Background and objectives

Significance → prior knowledge → gap → objective. Keep to 300–450 characters. Split into two sections only when the source material genuinely requires it.

## 04 Methods

Explain each major method's purpose, inputs, logic and assumptions. Insert original equation screenshots and define every symbol, subscript and unit. Reuse the source subsection names where they exist.

## 05 Results

**Subheadings must mirror the source Results section one-to-one** — same count, same order, same meaning, translated only. If the source has no subheadings, add none.

Present the main findings and cite every in-scope figure/table in the prose, including subpanels. One paragraph per figure: conclusion sentence → one or two numbers → (Fig. 1a).

Figure order: screenshot → Chinese title → complete Chinese notes.

Table order: Chinese title → **screenshot → complete Chinese notes**. The table body stays in the original language and is never rebuilt as a native Word table.

## 06 Discussion

If present, summarize each discussion theme's conclusion and reasoning chain, mirroring the source subheadings. Do not repeat result paragraphs.

## 07 Conclusions or policy implications

Follow the actual paper. Do not invent a conclusion section or repeat quantitative results.

## 08 Original English abstract

Copy the original abstract without paraphrasing. This outline is an English explanation; the default deliverable remains Chinese.
